Features

Everything a small business needs to stay on top of money

No spreadsheets, no accounting jargon. Just the tools you reach for every day, built to be fast on a phone.

Expenses & income

Record money in and out in a few taps. Sort it into categories and filter the list when you need to find something.

Invoices & quotations

Create a professional PDF with your logo, bank details, advances and discounts — then share it straight to WhatsApp or email.

Receipts, kept safe

Snap a receipt with your camera or pick one from your gallery and attach it to the expense. No more shoeboxes.

Multiple businesses

Run a shop and a side business? Keep each one separate, switch in a tap and compare them side by side.

Profit & loss

See revenue, expenses and net profit for any period, with a category breakdown that shows where the money goes.

Reports & tax export

Pull a clean summary when your accountant asks for it, instead of rebuilding the year from memory.

Clients & vendors

Keep the people you bill and buy from in one place, ready to drop onto the next invoice or quotation.

Light & dark mode

Easy on the eyes at the counter at noon and at the kitchen table at midnight. It follows your phone's setting.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is Petty Cash free?

Petty Cash is free to download from Google Play.

Which currencies are supported?

Ten: Sri Lankan rupee (the default), US dollar, euro, pound sterling, Indian rupee, Australian dollar, Canadian dollar, UAE dirham, Singapore dollar and Japanese yen. Each business can use its own currency.

Can I manage more than one business?

Yes. Add each business separately, switch between them from Settings and compare their performance side by side.

Is there an iPhone version?

Petty Cash is available on Android today. An iOS version is coming soon.

Can I put my own logo on invoices?

Yes. Upload a PNG or JPEG logo for each business, along with your payment details and footer line. They appear on every invoice and quotation PDF.
