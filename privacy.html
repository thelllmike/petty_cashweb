Privacy Policy

Petty Cash (com.pettycash.app) · Effective date: 4 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Petty Cash mobile app ("the app", "we", "us") collects, uses and protects your information. By using the app, you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

Account information. When you sign up, we collect your email address and a password, which is stored securely in hashed form. If you choose "Sign in with Google", we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google. We do not receive your Google password.

Business and financial records you enter. This includes businesses, expenses, income, categories, accounts, bank transactions, contacts (such as customer or supplier names and details), invoices and quotations.

Images you upload. This includes business logos, and receipt photos you take with the camera or pick from your device.

Technical data. We store the login session tokens needed to keep you signed in securely on your device.

We do not collect your location or contacts list, and we do not use advertising or analytics trackers.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To store, sync and display your business records across your devices.
  • To generate invoices, quotations, reports and exports that you request.
  • To send password-reset emails and important account messages.
  • To provide support and keep the service working properly.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Device permissions

  • Camera: used only when you choose to scan or photograph a receipt.
  • Photos / media: used only when you choose to upload a logo or receipt image.

4. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored with Supabase, our cloud database and authentication provider. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and protected by access rules, so each user can only access their own records. Google Sign-In is provided by Google.

Logo images may be stored at a public link so they can appear on invoices and quotations you share.

5. Sharing your information

We share data only:

  • with the service providers listed above, so the app can work;
  • when you choose to share a document (for example, sending an invoice PDF); or
  • when required by law.

6. Data retention and deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. You can edit or delete individual records in the app at any time.

To delete your account and all associated data, email us at sachinharshitha971@gmail.com from your registered email address. We will delete your account and data within 30 days.

7. Children's privacy

The app is intended for business use and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us at the address below.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

10. Contact us

If you have any questions about this policy, contact us at sachinharshitha971@gmail.com.
